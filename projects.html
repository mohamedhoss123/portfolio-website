VidFlow

Microservices-based video streaming platform

A NestJS gateway and a Go transcoding worker connected through gRPC and a Redis-backed BullMQ queue.

What I built

  • 100% non-blocking API responses by moving transcoding into background workers.
  • 1080p, 720p and 480p outputs delivered asynchronously.
  • Type-safe service calls with gRPC between NestJS and Go.
